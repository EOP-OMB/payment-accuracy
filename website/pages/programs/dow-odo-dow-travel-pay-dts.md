    controls, including inadequate segregation of duties in some cases, results in
    inconsistent review and approval processes, increasing the risk of improper payments.

    factors. The DIA identified instances of insufficient training for travelers,
    Organization Defense Travel Administrators (ODTAs), and Approving Officials (AOs)

    gap leads to documentation errors and improper payment approvals. DHA-MTF notes

    improper payments. The DHA-MTF identified missing information on who authorized

    system limitations, ultimately contributing to the overall rate of improper and
    unknown payments.
