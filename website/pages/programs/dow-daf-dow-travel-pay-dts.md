    established travel policies.

    supporting documentation. This is not a monetary loss but represents a significant
    compliance failure and a lack of transparency. The root cause of these unknown
    payments is a persistent failure to adhere to documentation standards, such as
    submitting reservation confirmations instead of final, itemized receipts, or neglecting
    to include mandatory justifications for verbal orders. These documentation deficiencies
    make it impossible to determine the propriety of the payments, highlighting a
    critical need for improved training, accountability, and system controls to ensure
    compliance with travel regulations.
