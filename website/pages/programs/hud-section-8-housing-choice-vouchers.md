    \ program.  Both ALNs/programs continued reporting improper payment estimates\
    \ in FY 2026. \n\nIn FY 2026, HUD targeted its estimation methodology toward transaction-level\
    \ testing and leveraged the results of the HUD's Office of the Inspector General's\
    \ (OIG) independent audit Report: 2026-FO-0006, HUD’s Public and Indian Housing\
    \ Tenant-Based Rental Assistance Program Had Significant Improper Payments, to\
    \ prepare its FY 2026 improper and unknown payment estimate. Through these audits,\
    \ HUD OIG sought to determine whether housing assistance payments (HAP) in the\
    \ PIH-TBRA programs are proper. HUD OIG selected a sample based on a one-month\
    \ population of active head of household program participants and tested payments\
    \ to verify tenant eligibility, payment amount, and compliance with HUD requirements.\
    \ HUD’s improper and unknown payment estimate was developed based upon OIG’s testing\
    \ results.\n\nA statistically valid one-month sample estimate was annualized (i.e.\
    \ multiplied by 12) to reach an estimate for the full year. The margin of error\
    \ of  2.32% is based on the statistically valid one-month sample estimate for\
    \ a combined PIH-TBRA HCV and PIH-TBRA Mainstream testing population.\n"
