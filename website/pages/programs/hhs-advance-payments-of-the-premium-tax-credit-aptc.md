  Corrective_Actions_Appropriateness: "HHS developed effective corrective actions\
    \ and mitigation strategies that are focused on identified root causes for improper\
    \ payments and plans to continue these actions in FY 2027.  The majority of overpayments\
    \ were due to manual errors. Corrective actions focused on staff training, audits,\
    \ and risk models to address the root causes of improper payments in FY 2027.\
    \ In addition, HHS also plans to use identifying and remediating system defects\
    \ as a mitigation strategy. \n\nAdditionally, Duplicate SSN errors made up a significant\
    \ portion of overpayments. To address these in July 2026, CMS extended rules already\
    \ in place for EDE agent/broker applications to enforce restrictions on agent/broker\
    \ assisted applications through other channels to ensure that verifiable SSNs\
    \ or immigration information must be provided on applications with an agent/broker\
    \ including through consumer applications on HealthCare.gov and the Marketplace\
    \ Call Center. Such applications are now required to include SSNs verified through\
    \ the Social Security Administration (SSA) for all individuals over 90 days old\
    \ who are requesting coverage (“applicants”). Through all application channels,\
    \ Agents have always been required to provide SSNs for applicants who have them,\
    \ but this system update will help CMS better enforce the requirement systematically\
    \ for all channels where agents and brokers can assist with applications, including\
    \ consumer applications on HealthCare.gov and when calling the Marketplace Call\
    \ Center. For any noncitizen applicants who do not have an SSN, information from\
    \ immigration documents that can be verified through the Department of Homeland\
    \ Security in real time will be required for application submission. Because for\
    \ an SSN to be verified by SSA it must match SSA records for the applicants name\
    \ and date of birth, we believe this change is likely to reduce the rate of duplicate\
    \ SSNs. In addition, we’ve made some additional specific technical changes to\
    \ our process for canceling what appear to be overlapping enrollments based on\
    \ matching demographic information. We believe these changes will also reduce\
    \ the occurrence of duplicate SSNs across policies."
