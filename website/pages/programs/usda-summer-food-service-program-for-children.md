  detail_IP_Causes: 'USDA reimburses participating Summer Food Service Program (SFSP)
    providers for the cost of serving program meals to eligible children. However,
    USDA does not reimburse providers directly. Instead, USDA issues payment to the
    states. The states then redistribute those funds to SFSP program sponsors. Sponsors
    use those funds to provide summer sites with meals for distribution to participant
    children.


    A significant source of program error is committed by household applicants for
    program benefits and the sponsors who process those applications. Sponsoring organizations
    also make errors in determining whether the sites under their supervision are
    located in area eligible and/or rural locations. Area eligibility allows sites
    to serve federally reimbursable meals to all children. Sites located in both area-eligible
    and rural locations can provide meals to children or their guardians for pickup
    or delivery. (All other SFSP sites serve meals for onsite consumption only).


    Additional error occurs in the process of counting and aggregating meals for federal
    reimbursement. Meal aggregation is a responsibility of summer site operators and
    program sponsors.'
