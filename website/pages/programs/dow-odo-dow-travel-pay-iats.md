  detail_IP_Causes: 'During the FY2026 Payment Integrity Information Act (PIIA) review,
    a statistically significant sample of 629 payments in the DoD Travel Pay ODO -
    IATS Program revealed an estimated improper plus unknown payment rate of 7.85%
    and an estimated $1.2M ($1,185,842) in improper plus unknown payments. This represents
    a significant reduction from the FY2025 projected estimates of $1.9M ($1,922,075)
    and a 13.05% error rate, demonstrating a major year-over-year improvement. The
    primary drivers were identified as a combination of unknown payments, overpayments,
    and underpayments resulting from calculation errors (wrong rate used to compute),
    missing supporting documentation, and processing oversight (entitlements paid
    that were not authorized).


    Due to the complex nature of the DoD Travel Pay ODO - IATS workload, high turnover
    rates for voucher examiners, and challenging production performance standards,
    processors made calculation errors based on incorrect rates, missing supporting
    documentation, and/or reimbursed unauthorized entitlements. This resulted from
    improper guidance being provided on "pre-computed" claims, processors failing
    to validate rates, processors failing to follow standard operating procedure (SOP)
    guidance when computing claims, and the customer submission of inaccurate and/or
    incomplete travel claim packets. These incomplete packets frequently resulted
    from customer order writing systems failing to generate orders that meet strict
    statutory requirements.


    The primary causes of Monetary Losses to the Agency (estimated at $567,984) were
    processors incorrectly overpaying TDY Meal and Incidental (M&IE) rates, projected
    at roughly $344,000, and reimbursing unallowed Civilian Relocation Real Estate
    expenses, projected at $76,000. Underpayments (estimated at $143,944) were similarly
    driven by calculation errors, primarily TDY M&IE being underpaid, which accounted
    for approximately $93,000 of the total.


    Statutorily Improper Payments (estimated at $277,166) were predominantly caused
    by customer submission of invalid or illegible documentation and invalid receipts,
    which combined for a projected $184,000 in statutory errors not caught by processors.
    Finally, Unknown Payments (estimated at $196,748) were primarily caused by an
    inability to complete post-pay reviews due to missing or conflicting information,
    accounting for roughly $130,000 of the unknown total.
