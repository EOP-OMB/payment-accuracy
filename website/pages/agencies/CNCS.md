detail_Executive_Summary: "As the federal agency for national service and volunteering,\
  \ AmeriCorps brings leadership, resources, coordination, focus, and scale to America’s\
  \ voluntary and service sector. In addition to meeting our mission and serving Americans\
  \ nationwide, the agency is committed to effective stewardship of federal resources.\n\
  \nIn FY 2026, AmeriCorps reported improper payment rates for two programs in Phase\
  \ 2 including AmeriCorps State and National (ASN) and the Foster Grandparent Program\
  \ (FGP). The scope of testing for ASN and FGP includes grantee partner payments\
  \ made in Q3 and Q4 of FY 2025 and Q1 and Q2 of FY 2026. None of the ASN or FGP\
  \ improper payments made are within the agency’s control and most improper payments\
  \ are due to inadequate record keeping and calculation errors. \n\nBoth programs\
  \ experienced reductions in their improper payment rates. The ASN program had a\
  \ slight decrease in its improper payment rate from 8.77% ($33.70M) in FY 2025 to\
  \ 8.09% ($28.04M) in FY 2026 which is attributable to variations in testing results\
  \ from random sampling. AmeriCorps observed a decrease in its FGP improper payment\
  \ rate from 23.60% ($22.15M) in FY 2025 to 17.08% ($12.96M) in FY 2026. \n\nThe\
  \ decreased rate in the FGP program was a result of improved compliance with National\
  \ Service Criminal History Checks (NSCHC) requirements. To help reduce rates further,\
  \ AmeriCorps has developed new monitoring activities that occur when a routine Payment\
  \ Integrity Information Act (PIIA) testing reveals improper payments. The discovery\
  \ of improper payments will trigger baseline or advanced monitoring, depending on\
  \ the grant recipient’s organizational risk level. Higher risk grantee partners\
  \ will receive advanced monitoring, while all others will receive baseline monitoring.\
  \ Baseline monitoring activities began in FY26, and advanced monitoring activities\
  \ will be implemented beginning in FY27. Grant recipients who have improper payments\
  \ related to National Service Criminal History Checks (NSCHC) are now required to\
  \ adopt the agency approved systems and vendors for conducting NSCHC checks. AmeriCorps\
  \ will continue to monitor grant recipient compliance with the training requirements\
  \ and terms and conditions and will continue to recover monies for collectable improper\
  \ payments. AmeriCorps did not implement changes in payment integrity methodologies\
  \ during FY 2026 for its reportable grant programs, AmeriCorps State and National\
  \ and the Foster Grandparent Program. \n\nAmeriCorps was non-compliant with two\
  \ of the annual Office of Inspector General (OIG) audit criteria, publishing accurate\
  \ and reliable rates for each of its susceptible programs and reporting improper\
  \ payment and unknown payment estimates of less than ten percent for FGP.  Additionally,\
  \ though AmeriCorps conducted a program-specific risk assessment, the OIG concluded\
  \ that AmeriCorps did not adequately conclude program risk as it relates to the\
  \ National Service Trust (NST). \n\nThe agency is committed to improving its payment\
  \ integrity process. AmeriCorps holds grantee partners accountable for found improper\
  \ payments by disallowing costs, requiring training for National Service Criminal\
  \ History Checks (NSCHC) and Funds Management, using information about improper\
  \ payments to inform the agency’s grant portfolio risk assessment, and providing\
  \ tools for grant recipients to improve. AmeriCorps has dramatically reduced its\
  \ improper payment rates in recent years via grantee partner accountability mechanisms\
  \ and compliance tools. The agency will continue to recover applicable improper\
  \ payments.\n"
detail_OIG_Recommendations: "AmeriCorps was non-compliant with two of the audit criteria,\
  \ publishing accurate and reliable rates for each of its susceptible programs and\
  \ reporting improper payment and unknown payment estimates of less than ten percent\
  \ for FGP.  Additionally, though AmeriCorps conducted a program-specific risk assessment,\
  \ the OIG concluded that AmeriCorps did not adequately conclude program risk as\
  \ it relates to the National Service Trust (NST). \n\nTo help reduce rates, AmeriCorps\
  \ has developed new monitoring activities that occur when a routine Payment Integrity\
  \ Information Act (PIIA) testing reveals improper payments. The discovery of improper\
  \ payments will trigger baseline or advanced monitoring, depending on the grantee’s\
  \ organizational risk level.  Higher risk grantees will receive advanced monitoring,\
  \ while all others will receive baseline monitoring. Baseline monitoring activities\
  \ began in FY26, and advanced monitoring activities will be implemented beginning\
  \ in FY27. Grantees who have improper payments related to National Service Criminal\
  \ History Checks (NSCHC) are now required to adopt the agency approved systems and\
  \ vendors for conducting NSCHC checks. AmeriCorps will continue to monitor grantee\
  \ compliance with the training requirements and terms and conditions and will continue\
  \ to recover monies for collectable improper payments.\n\nAmeriCorps had audit findings\
  \ that it did not publish accurate or valid rates for ASN or FGP because it does\
  \ not include differences between filed Federal Financial Reports and general ledgers\
  \ as improper payments. The OIG recommends AmeriCorps reconcile the difference between\
  \ grantees’ internal account records and Federal Financial Reports or to have the\
  \ grantees perform the reconciliation during the payment integrity testing cycle\
  \ and report unmatched errors as unknown payments. AmeriCorps does not agree that\
  \ unmatched reporting errors represent improper payments as grantees have until\
  \ the close of their award to reconcile their accounting records. AmeriCorps does\
  \ not concur with the findings that resulted in the reported noncompliance.\n\n\
  The agency also had audit findings that it did not adequately conclude program risk\
  \ as it relates to the National Service Trust (NST). AmeriCorps does not concur\
  \ with this notice of finding and recommendation. The agency conducted Trust testing\
  \ and published rates in FY 2024. The agency considers the rate statistically valid\
  \ and has determined that the Trust is not susceptible to Phase II testing because\
  \ the rate is below the statutory threshold. The extrapolated rate published was\
  \ 3.15%. The Trust does not rely solely on information reported by the AmeriCorps\
  \ State and National program, but also information reported by AmeriCorps VISTA\
  \ and AmeriCorps NCCC which are not deemed susceptible."
