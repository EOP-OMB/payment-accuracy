  reviews the list for items in need of adjudication.  Flagged payments or payments
  listed as possible matches are reviewed with the respective ACME division making
  the payment before a determination is reported to Treasury on the portal.  Since
  FY 2023, ACME has reviewed 10,000 payments that totaled $1 billion using the Do
  Not Pay Initiative and has neither identified a material amount nor realized a reduction
  of improper payments attributable to the implementing the Do Not Pay Initiative
  capabilities. Rather, ACME’s Do Not Pay Initiative implementation has proven that
