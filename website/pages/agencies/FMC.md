detail_DNP_Discussion: The Federal Maritime Commission (FMC), through its shared service
  provider, the U.S. Department of the Treasury’s Bureau of the Fiscal Service (BFS),
  utilizes the Do Not Pay (DNP) Working System to support payment verification and
  compliance with federal payment integrity requirements. The DNP Working System provides
  an additional layer of preventive controls by screening payment information against
  authoritative data sources to identify potential eligibility concerns and payment
  risks. Given the Commission’s historically low improper payment rate, the FMC considers
  DNP screening an important component of its overall internal control framework.
  The Commission continues to leverage this system, in conjunction with established
  pre-payment reviews and financial oversight procedures, to promote payment accuracy,
  accountability, and responsible stewardship of federal funds.

detail_Recovery_Not_Cost_Effective_Justification: The Federal Maritime Commission
  (FMC) has determined that conducting payment recovery audits is not cost-effective
  for its programs based on the agency's limited payment volume, historically low
  improper payment risk, and established internal control framework. The Commission's
  expenditures primarily consist of payroll, travel, contracts, and interagency agreements,
  which are subject to routine pre-payment reviews, financial reconciliations, and
  management oversight. Given the nature and volume of these transactions, the anticipated
  costs of conducting formal recovery audits, including administrative resources,
  personnel time, and audit-related expenses, are expected to exceed the potential
  recoverable amounts. The FMC continues to monitor payment activity, investigate
  identified discrepancies, and pursue recovery of overpayments when appropriate through
  existing financial management processes. Accordingly, the Commission considers its
  current risk-based monitoring and recovery procedures a more efficient and economical
  approach to safeguarding federal funds while maintaining compliance with applicable
  payment integrity requirements.
