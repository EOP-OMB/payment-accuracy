detail_DNP_Discussion: "Do Not Pay is a governmentwide tool provided by the U.S. Department\
  \ of the Treasury’s Bureau of the Fiscal Service for federal agencies and states\
  \ operating federally funded programs to check data sources to verify a recipient's\
  \ identity, payment and award eligibility, and bank account information before a\
  \ payment is made. As a one-stop shop for pre-award and pre-payment verification,\
  \ Do Not Pay helps agencies streamline their business operations to effectively\
  \ fight fraud, waste, and abuse in Federal spending. The Do Not Pay program is authorized\
  \ and governed by the Payment Integrity Information Act of 2019 (PIIA), codified\
  \ at 31 U.S.C. § 3351 et seq. Under this law, federal agencies must use Do Not Pay\
  \ to conduct pre-award and pre-payment verifications to determine program or award\
  \ eligibility and prevent improper payments before the release of any Federal funds.\
  \ PIIA also authorizes states that administer federally funded programs to utilize\
  \ Do Not Pay. Building on this foundation, Executive Order (EO) 14249, Protecting\
  \ America’s Bank Account Against Fraud, Waste, and Abuse, strengthened protections\
  \ against fraud, waste, and abuse by updating guidance to agencies and enhancing\
  \ Treasury systems to ensure all payments made by Treasury on behalf of agencies\
  \ are subject to pre-certification verification processes.\n\nIn accordance with\
  \ PIIA and EO 14249 requirements, DHS continued the legacy use of Do Not Pay capabilities,\
  \ primarily weekly and monthly bulk file batch matching and continuous monitoring,\
  \ throughout fiscal year (FY) 2026 for pre-award and pre-payment reviews and collaborated\
  \ in enhanced usage related to Treasury Payment Verification screening for daily\
  \ at the time of payment reviews. From October 2025 through September 2026, DHS\
  \ reviewed over $207 billion against Do Not Pay data sets. Note that the Department’s\
  \ FY 2026 disbursement activity was significantly elevated due to the processing\
  \ of validated refunds by the U.S. Customs and Border Protection for duties imposed\
  \ by International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA) tariff collections. Over\
  \ $189.9 billion has been screened through the Treasury Payment Verification screening\
  \ since soft launch on January 23, 2026. As a result of the Death Screening specifically,\
  \ DHS had a total of 330 payments totaling just over $1 million stopped by Treasury\
  \ in FY 2026 when definitively matched to the Social Security Administration’s death\
  \ records.\n\nIn recognition of the value of the Do Not Pay initiative in preventing\
  \ improper awards and payments, the Department plans to expand usage in FY 2027\
  \ to include but not limited to:\n•\tFull usage of the Treasury Payment Verification\
  \ screening to add Tax Identification Number (TIN) screening and bank account validation,\
  \ both launched on September 30, 2026.\n•\tMigration of DHS’s legacy batch matching\
  \ and continuous monitoring capabilities to Do Not Pay Connect capabilities, to\
  \ include direct system interconnections and bi-directional file transfers.\n•\t\
  Increased user access and usage of the Do Not Pay Web Portal to conduct single and\
  \ multiple eligibility searches for pre-award and pre-payment verification while\
  \ also enabling review and adjudication efforts for risks identified through Do\
  \ Not Pay Connect. \n"
