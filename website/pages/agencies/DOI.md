detail_Executive_Summary: The Payment Integrity Information Act of 2019 (PIIA), enacted
  on March 2, 2020 (P.L. 116-117), consolidated previous improper payment laws and
  strengthened federal requirements for identifying and reducing improper payments.
  OMB further clarified these requirements in Memorandum M-21-19, issued on March
  5, 2021, which outlines agency responsibilities for conducting Phase 1 risk assessments
  and, where applicable, Phase 2 statistical testing. More recently, Executive Orders
  14249 and 14247, signed on March 25, 2025, further emphasized the importance of
  payment integrity by directing agencies to modernize payment systems, reduce fraud,
  and transition to electronic payments. In alignment with PIIA, the Department of
  the Interior (DOI) conducts comprehensive risk assessments on a three-year cycle
  for all programs with annual outlays exceeding $10 million. With OMB concurrence,
  DOI uses an alternative annual assessment period spanning from Q4 of the prior fiscal
  year through Q3 of the current fiscal year. In FY 2025, DOI assessed 103 programs
  totaling approximately $33.31 billion in payments. None of the programs were identified
  as being at high risk of susceptibility to significant improper payments, and no
  new programs were required to proceed to Phase 2 testing. DOI remains compliant
  with PIIA requirements and continues to monitor programs in off-cycle years. Additionally,
  DOI will conduct off-cycle risk assessments if a program undergoes significant changes
  such as new legislation, substantial funding increases, structural changes, or at
  the request of management that may elevate its risk of improper payments.
