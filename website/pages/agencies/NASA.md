detail_Executive_Summary: 'The NASA Office of Inspector General (OIG), in its FY 2025
  review of NASA’s compliance with the Payment Integrity Information Act (Report IG-26-009),
  concluded that NASA complied with PIIA requirements. This finding provides assurance
  that NASA’s payment integrity processes and controls operate in accordance with
  applicable federal requirements.


  NASA conducted its FY 2026 Payment Integrity Risk Assessment in accordance with
  OMB Circular A-123, Appendix C, Requirements for Payment Integrity Improvement.
  NASA annually updates its risk assessment methodology to reflect changes in federal
  laws and guidance, the Agency’s operating environment, external audit recommendations,
  and other relevant factors. For FY 2026, NASA’s methodology incorporated seven risk
  conditions aligned with the eleven OMB-designated risk factors. Consistent with
  OMB guidance (M-21-19), programs with annual outlays exceeding $10 million are classified
  as either Phase 1 or Phase 2 based on their likelihood of exceeding the statutory
  threshold for improper and unknown payments. NASA currently has no Phase 2 programs.


  For the FY 2026 assessment, NASA reviewed $27.25 billion in FY 2025 disbursements
  and identified 82 programs with annual outlays exceeding $10 million. As part of
  the Agency’s triennial full assessment cycle, NASA conducted risk assessments for
  all 82 programs, regardless of whether they were assessed in the prior two years.
  In off-cycle years, NASA assesses newly identified programs that exceed the $10
  million threshold, as well as existing programs with significant changes that may
  warrant an off-cycle assessment. None of the programs were found to be susceptible
  to significant improper payments. NASA maintains payment integrity through strong
  internal controls including both preventative controls (e.g., payment approval processes)
  and detective controls (e.g., the continuous monitoring program).'
