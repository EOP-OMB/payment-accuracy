      \ Circular A-133. \n\nHUD also conducts program oversight through the Section\
      \ Eight Management Assessment Program (SEMAP), a system used by HUD to measure\
      \ the performance of Public Housing Agencies (PHAs) that administer the Housing\
      \ Choice Voucher (HCV) Program.\n"
