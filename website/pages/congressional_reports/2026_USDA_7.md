- Answer: Performance agreements related to this topic have been developed for the
    accountable official(s) within FNA and FSA. FSA incorporated accountability mechanisms
    into performance plans for supervisory and non-supervisory positions. FSA leadership
    will collaborate to hold employees accountable for repetitive errors affecting
    the improper payment error rate. This includes evaluation of the source of the
    errors (i.e., the state and county) and identifying employees responsible. Supervisors
    will be held accountable to document the deficiencies in the employees’ performance
    evaluations. FSA’s efforts to improve improper error rates includes accountability
    measures in performance management, ensuring business process improvements such
    as new software development and enhancements include appropriate internal controls
    for pre- and post-payment checks; and monitoring payment integrity utilizing a
    payment integrity dashboard.
