- Answer: "AmeriCorps was non-compliant with two of the audit criteria, publishing\
    \ accurate and reliable rates for each of its susceptible programs and reporting\
    \ improper payment and unknown payment estimates of less than ten percent for\
    \ FGP.  Additionally, though AmeriCorps conducted a program-specific risk assessment,\
    \ the OIG concluded that AmeriCorps did not adequately conclude program risk as\
    \ it relates to the National Service Trust (NST). \n\nTo help reduce rates, AmeriCorps\
    \ has developed new monitoring activities that occur when a routine Payment Integrity\
    \ Information Act (PIIA) testing reveals improper payments. The discovery of improper\
    \ payments will trigger baseline or advanced monitoring, depending on the grantee’s\
    \ organizational risk level.  Higher risk grantees will receive advanced monitoring,\
    \ while all others will receive baseline monitoring. Baseline monitoring activities\
    \ began in FY26, and advanced monitoring activities will be implemented beginning\
    \ in FY27. Grantees who have improper payments related to National Service Criminal\
    \ History Checks (NSCHC) are now required to adopt the agency approved systems\
    \ and vendors for conducting NSCHC checks. AmeriCorps will continue to monitor\
    \ grantee compliance with the training requirements and terms and conditions and\
    \ will continue to recover monies for collectable improper payments.\n\nAmeriCorps\
    \ had audit findings that it did not publish accurate or valid rates for ASN or\
    \ FGP because it does not include differences between filed Federal Financial\
    \ Reports and general ledgers as improper payments. The OIG recommends AmeriCorps\
    \ reconcile the difference between grantees’ internal account records and Federal\
    \ Financial Reports or to have the grantees perform the reconciliation during\
    \ the payment integrity testing cycle and report unmatched errors as unknown payments.\
    \ AmeriCorps does not agree that unmatched reporting errors represent improper\
    \ payments as grantees have until the close of their award to reconcile their\
    \ accounting records. AmeriCorps does not concur with the findings that resulted\
    \ in the reported noncompliance.\n\nThe agency also had audit findings that it\
    \ did not adequately conclude program risk as it relates to the National Service\
    \ Trust (NST). AmeriCorps does not concur with this notice of finding and recommendation.\
    \ The agency conducted Trust testing and published rates in FY 2024. The agency considers\
    \ the rate statistically valid and has determined that the Trust is not susceptible\
    \ to Phase II testing because the rate is below the statutory threshold. The extrapolated\
    \ rate published was 3.15%. The Trust does not rely solely on information reported\
    \ by the AmeriCorps State and National program, but also information reported\
    \ by AmeriCorps VISTA and AmeriCorps NCCC which are not deemed susceptible."
