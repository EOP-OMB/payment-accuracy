- Answer: 'Federal personnel within DOT’s Payment Integrity Center performed the recovery
    audit, focusing on DOT payments made from April 2025 through March 2026. Administrative
    errors occurring within the payment process are the primary cause of these overpayments.
    To resolve errors originating in systems outside the Federal internal control
    system, DOT program and financial management officials work directly with payees.


    DOT considers all overpayments identified through the FY 2026 audit to be recoverable.
    The Enterprise Service Center (ESC), DOT’s service provider, typically recoups
    overpayments resulting from DOT errors by collecting directly from the payee,
    offsetting the payee’s future payments, or referring the debt to the Department
    of the Treasury''s Offset Program. In most cases, ESC successfully recovers these
    overpayments directly from the payees.'
