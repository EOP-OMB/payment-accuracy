- Answer: "Summary of DoW OIG Non-Compliance Findings\n\nThe DoW was noncompliant\
    \ with the PIIA in its FY 2025 reporting of improper and unknown payments. Although\
    \ the DoW met three requirements, it did not:\n\n1.\tConduct sufficient risk assessments\
    \ to determine the risk of improper and unknown payments across all programs;\n\
    \n2.\tPublish reliable improper and unknown payment estimates; or\n\n3.\tMaintain\
    \ reported improper and unknown payment rates below the required 10 percent threshold\
    \ for applicable programs.\n\nThe remaining deficiencies reflect opportunities\
    \ to further mature established corrective actions. Specifically, DoW personnel\
    \ need to more consistently document how program-specific risk factors inform\
    \ risk determinations, complete actions to identify all programs requiring risk\
    \ assessment; and strengthen controls over the completeness and scope of payment-transaction\
    \ universes used to calculate improper and unknown payment estimates.\n\nThe work\
    \ completed to date provides a foundation for sustained improvement. Continued\
    \ implementation of corrective actions will strengthen risk assessment and estimation\
    \ processes, improve the reliability and transparency of reported results, reduce\
    \ improper and unknown payments, and position the DoW to achieve full PIIA compliance.\n\
    \nSummary of Actions Planned to Achieve Compliance\n\nDoW’s planned actions focus\
    \ on strengthening program ownership and oversight, improving risk assessments\
    \ and payment-data reliability, reducing high travel-payment error rates through\
    \ targeted controls, and submitting required remediation plans to the OMB.\n\n\
    •\tStrengthen portfolio governance and accountability. The DoW is continuing to\
    \ manage its Payment Integrity portfolio as smaller, more defined payment populations,\
    \ enabling components to assume clearer responsibility for risk assessments, corrective\
    \ actions, and results. The Under Secretary of War (Comptroller)/Chief Financial\
    \ Officer has also established measurable compliance milestones, assigned senior\
    \ executive ownership, and implemented accountability mechanisms for affected\
    \ programs by September 2027.\n\n•\tComplete and improve risk assessments. Planned\
    \ work includes documenting program-specific risks and the internal controls used\
    \ to mitigate them; refining risk-assessment methodologies; and completing overdue\
    \ assessments for the DoW Travel Pay Army–Integrated Automated Travel System (IATS)\
    \ during the FY 2026 audit cycle, and for Commercial Pay–Defense Agencies Initiative\
    \ and Commercial Pay–Computerized Accounts Payable System during the FY 2027 audit\
    \ cycle. \n\n•\tImprove the reliability of improper and unknown payment estimates.\
    \ The DoW plans to implement a Department-wide methodology to identify all outlays,\
    \ associate them with the correct programs, and verify that each program’s payment\
    \ universe is complete. This includes moving toward transaction-level reconciliations\
    \ and establishing a process to analyze variances in payment-universe data. The\
    \ reported target for completing this work is June 2027. \n\n•\tReduce travel-payment\
    \ error rates below 10 percent. The DoW is implementing targeted corrective actions\
    \ across its travel-payment programs to reduce improper and unknown payment rates\
    \ below the 10 percent threshold. These efforts emphasize strengthened internal\
    \ controls, clearer accountability, improved documentation, enhanced training,\
    \ automated validation capabilities, and recurring management oversight.\n\nCollectively,\
    \ these actions are intended to improve payment accuracy, strengthen compliance,\
    \ and support sustained reductions in improper and unknown payments by September\
    \ 2027.\n\n•\tMeet external reporting and remediation requirements. By November\
    \ 30, 2026, the DoW plans to submit OMB-required compliance actions for identified\
    \ Commercial Pay and Travel Pay programs; submit additional program-integrity\
    \ proposals if applicable for Civilian Pay–ODO, Commercial Pay–Mechanization of\
    \ Contract Administration Services, and Army–DTS; and identify the related statutory\
    \ mitigation reports in the AFR and on PaymentAccuracy.gov.\n"
