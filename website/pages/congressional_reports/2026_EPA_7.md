- Answer: "In its August 2026 report (No. 26-P-0046), the EPA Office of Inspector\
    \ General (OIG) determined that EPA did not comply with the Payment Integrity\
    \ Information Act of 2019 for fiscal year (FY) 2025 reporting. EPA met three of\
    \ the four compliance requirements: publishing payment integrity information,\
    \ posting the information on the agency website, and conducting the required risk\
    \ assessments. However, the OIG states EPA did not satisfy the fourth requirement\
    \ because the agency did not adequately determine whether two payment streams\
    \ were likely to make improper and unknown payments above or below the statutory\
    \ threshold.\n\nSpecifically, the OIG found that EPA did not maintain sufficient\
    \ documentation to support the risk assessment conclusions for the Grants and\
    \ Clean School Bus grants payment streams. The OIG also found that EPA did not\
    \ document how it considered known program risks, including prior OIG findings,\
    \ in conducting the Clean School Bus grants payment stream risk assessment. To\
    \ address these issues, the OIG recommended that EPA strengthen its risk assessment\
    \ methodology, improve testing guidance and training, and obtain additional support\
    \ to resolve questioned costs and enhance future compliance.\n\nEPA acknowledges\
    \ the OIG’s findings and appreciates the shared commitment to payment integrity,\
    \ while respectfully differing with the conclusion that the Agency lacked sufficient\
    \ documentation to assess susceptibility to significant improper payments. In\
    \ its September 28, 2026, audit response, EPA noted that Uniform Grants Guidance\
    \ does not prescribe the format of recipient source documentation and reaffirmed\
    \ its commitment to strengthening internal controls in support of mission delivery\
    \ and Administrator priorities.  \n\nEPA has completed corrective actions on four\
    \ of five compliance-related recommendations, including implementing program-specific\
    \ qualitative risk assessments, updating standard operating procedures to incorporate\
    \ OIG-recommended elements and consideration of prior OIG work, and revising transaction\
    \ testing guidance to better align with 2 C.F.R. 200. The final corrective action\
    \ is expected to be completed in November 2026."
