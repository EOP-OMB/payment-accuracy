- Answer: 'For FY2025, OIG concluded that USAGM did not comply with applicable PIIA
    payment integrity requirements because the Agency did not publish the required
    payment integrity disclosures with its FY2025 financial statements or in the required
    accompanying materials and did not include the required link to PaymentAccuracy.gov
    in its FY2025 Agency Financial Report. OIG attributed these reporting deficiencies,
    in part, to reductions in USAGM’s workforce and organizational changes that affected
    the Agency’s financial reporting environment and its ability to prepare a complete
    Agency Financial Report. OIG nevertheless found that USAGM conducted the required
    program-specific risk assessments and made appropriate conclusions regarding whether
    the assessed programs exceeded the applicable statutory improper-payment thresholds,
    and OIG did not identify needed improvements related to the Agency’s improper
    and unknown payment determinations or prevention and reduction efforts. OIG made
    one recommendation to improve USAGM’s compliance with PIIA, and, based on USAGM’s
    response to the draft report, OIG considered the recommendation resolved pending
    further action. In response, USAGM is reestablishing and strengthening the processes
    necessary to collect, review, and report required payment integrity information,
    improving coordination among responsible offices, and addressing the payment integrity
    reporting requirements applicable to USAGM for FY2026. As measurable FY2026 milestones,
    USAGM is completing its required three-year-cycle risk assessment activities,
    preparing the required payment integrity disclosures and accompanying materials,
    coordinating their incorporation into the FY2026 Agency Financial Report, and
    ensuring that the required reference and link to PaymentAccuracy.gov are included.
    USAGM’s corrective actions are intended to address each reporting deficiency identified
    by OIG and establish sufficient processes and resources to consistently meet applicable
    PIIA reporting requirements. The Agency’s goal is to complete these corrective
    actions as part of the FY2026 reporting cycle and achieve compliance with applicable
    payment integrity requirements.
