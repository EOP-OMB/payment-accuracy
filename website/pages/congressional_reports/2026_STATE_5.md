- Answer: "The Department of State's Bureau of the Comptroller and Global Financial\
    \ Services (CGFS) provides financial management services to the Department and\
    \ other agencies. CGFS monitors improper payments through a two-tiered review\
    \ process. First, payment-issuing offices review every payment before disbursement.\
    \ Second, CGFS’ Office of Governance and Oversight (CGFS/GOV) conducts internal\
    \ post-payment audits, using random and targeted samples of domestic, overseas,\
    \ and employee payments. \n\nDomestic vendor payments make up the largest category\
    \ of Department payments subject to Payment Integrity Information Act (PIIA) recovery\
    \ audit requirements. Each month, CGFS/GOV runs an automated comparison of vendor\
    \ invoice data against prior-year payments and flags potential duplicates for\
    \ recapture audit analysis. This automated process has proven a cost-effective\
    \ way to review domestic payments. Each quarter, CGFS/GOV also manually reviews\
    \ samples of domestic and overseas payments. It reviews supporting documentation\
    \ to confirm that the Department paid the correct vendor the correct amount. \n\
    \nCGFS/GOV also audits employee payments each quarter, including permanent change\
    \ of station (PCS) travel, temporary duty (TDY) travel, tuition and education\
    \ allowances, representation expenses, local transportation, and other reimbursements.\
    \ These audits review supporting documentation to confirm that payments are accurate\
    \ and comply with regulations. \n\nHistorically, the most common causes of potential\
    \ improper payments were caused by insufficient supporting documentation or a\
    \ misinterpretation of applicable regulations. Generally, only minimal amounts\
    \ have been referred for recapture because of recovery audits. In FY 2026, the\
    \ Department of State identified $0 in improper payments through recovery audits.\
    \ \n\nCGFS continues to review these processes and, where possible, uses automation\
    \ and super intelligence (SI) tools to make them more efficient and effective."
