- Answer: "In August 2026, HUD's Office of the Inspector General (OIG) reported that\
    \ HUD did not comply with the Payment Integrity Information Act (PIIA) in fiscal\
    \ year (FY) 2025. HUD did not comply with PIIA because it reported an unknown\
    \ payment estimate for Public and Indian Housing (PIH) Tenant-Based Rental Assistance\
    \ (TBRA) and  Multifamily Housing (MFH) Project-Based Rental Assistance (PBRA)\
    \ programs based on data matching but did not perform enough testing to report\
    \ compliant improper payment and unknown payment estimates for the programs. As\
    \ a result, HUD could not effectively implement corrective actions and other OMB\
    \ requirements for programs above the statutory threshold that are designed to\
    \ improve payment accuracy. Finally, OIG determined that HUD did not follow the\
    \ Do Not Pay Initiative (DNP), as required by PIIA because it did not consistently\
    \ use DNP for prepayment verification.  \n\nHUD acknowledges that additional work\
    \ remains to achieve PIIA compliance but believes its FY 2025 approach provided\
    \ visibility into risks that had not previously been evaluated at this scale and\
    \ enabled identification of $5.8 billion in potential payment errors across its\
    \ highest-risk programs. HUD reported substantial progress in validating those\
    \ results, resolving 97.7 percent of deceased tenant cases, 98.1 percent of nonconforming\
    \ Social Security Number cases, and 40.8 percent of inactive SAM.gov registration\
    \ issues through transaction-level reviews, recipient outreach, system updates,\
    \ and refined business rules (in response to recommendations 2026-FO-0004-2A,\
    \ 2B, 2C, 2E & 2F). Many flagged items reflected administrative delays, outdated\
    \ records, and business rule-related data discrepancies rather than true improper\
    \ payments or monetary loss. To support future compliance, HUD implemented the\
    \ Rental Assistance Data Accuracy & Risk (RADAR) dashboard and SAM.gov Unique\
    \ Entity Identifier Validation Automation to strengthen monitoring, improve data\
    \ quality, and enable earlier detection of payment integrity risks. HUD is addressing\
    \ OIG's findings through stronger governance, improved testing and estimation\
    \ methodologies, expanded coordination across OCFO, PIH, and MFH, enhanced monitoring,\
    \ and automation designed to improve error detection, validation, and prevention.\
    \ As a measurable milestone, HUD leveraged the results of OIG’s independent Report:\
    \ 2026-FO-0006, HUD’s Public and Indian Housing Tenant-Based Rental Assistance\
    \ Program Had Significant Improper Payments, and Report: 2026-BO-0002, HUD’s Multifamily\
    \ Project-Based Rental Assistance Program Had Significant Improper Payments, to\
    \ prepare its estimate for payment integrity reporting purposes. HUD also remains\
    \ on track to complete Department-wide Do Not Pay implementation and supporting\
    \ policy updates needed to strengthen ongoing PIIA compliance efforts (in response\
    \ to recommendations 2026-FO-0004-2D & 2G).\n"
