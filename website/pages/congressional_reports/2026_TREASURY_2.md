  - Agency: TREASURY
    Answer: 'The IRS implemented targeted corrective actions across its refundable
      tax credit programs to reduce improper and unknown payments. These efforts,
      completed or planned for completion by the fourth quarter of FY 2026, reflect
      a coordinated, data-driven approach to improving payment accuracy across the
      Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC), Additional Child Tax Credit (ACTC), American
      Opportunity Tax Credit (AOTC), and Net Premium Tax Credit (Net PTC) programs.


      To strengthen compliance, the IRS conducted its annual Dependent Database (DDb)
      meeting with stakeholders from Taxpayer Services, Small Business/Self-Employed,
      and Information Technology to review prior-year results and refine compliance
      filters used to select returns for examination. The IRS also leveraged cross-enterprise
      data sharing with federal partners, including the Office of Child Support Services
      (OCSS), the Social Security Administration (SSA), and the Centers for Medicare
      & Medicaid Services (CMS), to verify eligibility and identify discrepancies
      before refunds were issued.


      The IRS also continued providing annual due diligence training for tax professionals
      through the IRS Nationwide Tax Forums and filing season readiness webinars.
      These outreach efforts help improve understanding of refundable tax credit eligibility
      requirements, support accurate return preparation, and address improper payments
      caused by taxpayer or preparer error. Together, these actions demonstrate the
      IRS''s commitment to continuously improving payment integrity while minimizing
      burden on taxpayers.'

- Answer: "Many of Treasury's overpayments are payroll related. These overpayments\
    \ include issues such as corrected timecards, Federal Employees Group Life Insurance\
    \ corrections, duplicate cash awards, etc. These conditions are identified and\
    \ resolved by utilizing mitigation strategies and corrective actions that are\
    \ appropriate to the severity of the error, such as post payment reviews.\n\n\
    Accounts payable, pension, grants, and loan payments use pre-payment and post\
    \ payment reviews to recoup overpayments.  Invoices processed through the Invoice\
    \ Processing Platform (IPP) are subject to a monthly post payment audit of 10%\
    \ of the total paid IPP invoices for that month. Invoices not processed through\
    \ the IPP in excess of $2,500.00 are subject to secondary review before being\
    \ paid. Invoices under $2,500.00 are subject to review through statistical sampling\
    \ procedures. Travel vouchers processed through the Concur Government Edition\
    \ (CGE) travel system are subject to a post payment audit based on a random sample\
    \ of all reimbursements under $2,500.00.  All payments $2,500.00 require a 100%\
    \ post payment audit review.  Relocation vouchers processed through the moveLINQ\
    \ system are subject to 100% pre-payment audit and review.  Relocation periodically\
    \ reconciles moveLINQ advances (feeder) to Oracle (financial system) advances\
    \ to ensure the two systems are in sync. They validate W-2's to ensure estimated\
    \ accruals reconcile to actual accruals to verify the correct amount of taxes\
    \ were deducted. Accruals are balanced by accounting string under general ledger\
    \ to ensure accruals collected were paid. Relocation also balances moveLINQ to\
    \ Oracle each tax period to ensure wages and taxes reconcile between the two systems.\
    \ They also verify outstanding balances and billed amounts on obligations within\
    \ the moveLINQ system reconciles to outstanding balances and billed amounts in\
    \ Oracle. These efforts confirm there are no duplicate payments and taxes are\
    \ collected and paid at the appropriate rate. \n\nFurthermore, the Do Not Pay\
    \ (DNP) portal implementation has provided a way for bureau's/office's procurement\
    \ and payable offices to review vendor files prior to disbursement. It has also\
    \ given them an opportunity to review the monthly payment file results to identify\
    \ any improper payments made to a vendor in the DNP portal.\n"
