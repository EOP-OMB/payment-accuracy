  Text: (1) NONCOMPLIANCE.—If an executive agency is determined by the Inspector General

  Type: REGULAR

    \ that will support each organizational performance goal. As an example “Supports\

    \ audit opinion.” SES members with a direct role must comply with the policies\

    \ submit auditor-requested materials timely, remediate high risk/priority material\

    \ process and internal control improvements, identifies root cause(s) of issues\

    \ are accountable for driving government efficiencies within their organization\
    \ to streamline proficiencies, quality work and cost-effective use of government\
