      of Proceeds:  CDCs did not always ensure that 504 proceeds complied with program
      requirements.  In one change of ownership transaction, the acquisition cost
      exceeded the allowable amount, limited to the lower of the appraised ''as-is''
      value or the actual purchase price, and the CDC submitted a loan modification
      to resolve the issue.  In another case, a debt refinance was ineligible because
      a significant portion of the existing debt had not been used to acquire Eligible
      Fixed Assets.'
